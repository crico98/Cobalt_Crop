Guidance

  • Box the visible diamond face tightly.
  • Do not include carbide body or pocket.
  • Multiple visible faces can be boxed in one image.
  • Wear, chips, and spalling are acceptable if the face is locatable.
  • Skip faces that are broken, obscured, or too unclear to locate.
